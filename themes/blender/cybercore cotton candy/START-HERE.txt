Open cybercore-cotton-candy.html in your browser.
It works offline and contains both complete Blender theme downloads.

To install a theme:
1. In Blender, open Edit > Preferences > Themes.
2. Click Install... and choose Cybercore_Cotton_Candy_Soft.xml
   or Cybercore_Cotton_Candy_Vivid.xml.
3. Save Preferences if Auto-Save Preferences is off.

The HTML is the preview; Blender installs the XML file.
Both presets were validated with Blender 5.2.3 LTS.
Your saved Blender preferences have not been changed.

Soft and Vivid share the original pastel interface. Vivid uses richer
orchid and violet syntax and node-header colors.

Open README.md for the full little tour, palette, and compatibility notes.
