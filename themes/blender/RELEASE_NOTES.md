# ♡ CyberStudios Blender Themes · 1.0.0

**dream in pixels. create without limits.**

A little wardrobe for your 3D workspace: **five new soft feminine themes** and **five new grounded masculine themes**. The original ten presets are still included, bringing the collection to **twenty presets**.

## 🎀 new soft feminine palettes

- **Mint Meringue** · Light · Pale mint, sea-glass green, and a puff of cream. Cool, airy light for a clean and gentle workspace.
- **Blush Chiffon** · Light · Dusty rose, warm mauve, and a drift of blush chiffon. Soft and elegant, with a quiet romantic streak.
- **Sakura Dusk** · Dark · Deep rosewood evening with blossom-pink light. A tender night room for late sessions.
- **Sky Sorbet** · Light · Powder blue, cloud white, and a scoop of periwinkle. Bright daytime air for open, breezy scenes.
- **Butter Bloom** · Light · Buttercream, honey, and a petal of rose. Sunny warmth for a cheerful and cozy desk.

## ⌘ new grounded masculine palettes

- **Iron Oxide** · Dark · Deep umber, forged iron, and a rust-orange spark. A heavy, hard-working shop for big builds.
- **Deep Current** · Dark · Petrol teal, cold-water blue, and sea-foam highlights. A focused deep-water workspace.
- **Desert Sandstone** · Light · Sun-baked sand, khaki, and a deep clay accent. Warm, dry light with firm edges.
- **Carbon Volt** · Dark · Neutral near-black with a sharp electric lime edge. Crisp and fast for long technical sessions.
- **Ridgeline** · Light · Misty granite, fir green, and cold mountain air. Calm daylight with a steady forest accent.

## 💾 install

Download **CyberStudios-Blender-Themes-1.0.0.zip**, extract it, and install any XML through **Edit → Preferences → Themes → Install…**. Only XML files are needed in Blender.

The **Source ZIP** contains the editable projects, readmes, build tools, preview illustrations, and an offline HTML gallery with all twenty XML downloads embedded.

## 🫧 validation

All twenty presets passed Blender 5.2.3 LTS native import/export checks. The eighteen catalog palettes also passed their recorded text-contrast checks. Previews are illustrations; other Blender versions are untested.
