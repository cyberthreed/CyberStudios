# ♡ CyberStudios Blender Themes · 1.0.0

**dream in pixels. create without limits.**

A little wardrobe for your 3D workspace: **four new cute themes**, **four new masculine themes**, and the original **Cotton Candy Soft and Vivid** presets.

## 🎀 cute palettes

- **Strawberry Milk** · Light · Baby pink, vanilla cream, and berry-colored details. A little strawberry sweetness between the panels.
- **Lavender Macaron** · Light · Whipped lilac, periwinkle, and soft plum. A tidy little patisserie for your polygons.
- **Peach Parfait** · Light · Peach, vanilla, and a spoonful of sage. Warm light for the next little world on your screen.
- **Moonlit Bunny** · Dark · A sleepy plum night with lilac, pink, and starlight-blue accents. Cute has a night room, too.

## ⌘ masculine palettes

- **Obsidian Forge** · Dark · Warm charcoal, bronze, and pale steel. A solid workbench for heavy ideas and careful detail.
- **Steel Horizon** · Dark · Midnight navy, brushed blue, and cool silver. Clear lines for a long view of the project.
- **Timberline** · Dark · Deep pine, olive, and weathered brass. A quiet cabin for complex scenes.
- **Concrete Day** · Light · Stone, graphite, and a restrained slate-blue accent. Bright, deliberate, and ready for the next build.

## 💾 install

Download **CyberStudios-Blender-Themes-1.0.0.zip**, extract it, and install any XML through **Edit → Preferences → Themes → Install…**. Only XML files are needed in Blender.

The **Source ZIP** contains the editable projects, readmes, build tools, preview illustrations, and an offline HTML gallery with all ten XML downloads embedded.

## 🫧 validation

All ten presets passed Blender 5.2.3 LTS native import/export checks. The eight new palettes also passed their recorded text-contrast checks. Previews are illustrations; other Blender versions are untested.
