✧ welcome to the ascii apparition exhibition

A little gallery of digital ghosts, animated characters, and artwork made from the things hiding underneath your keyboard.

This is where I experiment with ASCII art, turning letters, symbols, and code into something that feels more alive than it probably should. Some pieces are delicate. Some are strange. Some might look like they crawled out of a forgotten computer.

Take a look around. You never know what might appear next. ♡

COLLECTION A · ONGOING SERIES

ASCII Apparition Exhibition

A numbered, ongoing series of experimental ASCII artworks. Each apparition is a complete piece with its own number, and new entries are added to the end of the series.

#00

THE MACHINE DREAMS OF ANGELS

An interactive, animated ASCII exhibition that translates a gothic sigilism illustration into thousands of individually rendered characters: wings, long hair, an ornate sword, and thorn-covered linework, reconstructed through code.

even sleeping machines remember the sky.
