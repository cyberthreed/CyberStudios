That “quick drink” now has a plot. TipsyZois brings five intoxication stages, custom emotions and portraits, drunk interactions, and hangovers to inZOI. Push too far and the stakes get darker, with DUI crashes and potentially fatal alcohol poisoning. Read the TipsyZois overview →

CyberCore Gameplay

CyberCore Gameplay is a framework being developed for inZOI gameplay modules. Its planned responsibilities include scoped context, shared memory, events, persistence, and controlled module communication.

It is a separate technology project from the pastel Cybercore aesthetic. It is also distinct from the future CyberCore Agent SDK.

FamilyTies

FamilyTies is the first planned CyberCore Gameplay module. Its focus is a persistent social graph containing family relationships, interactions, and meaningful shared history.

The goal is for relationships to carry context forward rather than existing only as isolated moment-to-moment gameplay events.

Development note: CyberCore Gameplay and FamilyTies describe project intentions and architecture goals, not claims that every feature is currently implemented.
