⌘ gameplay_systems.txt inZOI MODS & SYSTEMS

Gameplay systems

TipsyZois · Released

That “quick drink” now has a plot. TipsyZois brings five intoxication stages, custom emotions and portraits, drunk interactions, and hangovers to inZOI. Push too far and the stakes get darker, with DUI crashes and potentially fatal alcohol poisoning. Read the TipsyZois overview →

CyberCore Gameplay

CyberCore Gameplay explores a larger question: how can simulation systems carry context from one moment to the next? The developing framework is planned around shared memory, events, persistence, and communication between compatible inZOI modules.

It is a separate technology project from the pastel Cybercore aesthetic. It is also distinct from the future CyberCore Agent SDK.

FamilyTies

FamilyTies is a developing CyberCore Gameplay module about the history between people. Its planned social graph brings family connections, past interactions, and shared memories into a relationship's context.

The ambition is a world where a relationship has a past. Family connections and meaningful encounters would have context to carry into what happens next.

Development note: CyberCore Gameplay and FamilyTies describe project intentions and architecture goals, not claims that every feature is currently implemented.
