Drink with TipsyZois.

Your Zoi went out for one drink. We all know how that story goes.

TipsyZois brings a full intoxication system to inZOI's alcoholic mixed drinks. Five escalating stages, custom emotions and portraits, drunk interactions, and hangovers give a night out a story of its own. Keep drinking, and that story can take a much darker turn.

Made for chaotic parties, complicated characters, and saves where the morning after deserves a chapter too. ♡

A night with TipsyZois ♡

🍸 Five stages. How did we get here?

It starts with the mixed drinks already in your game. Each additional drink, while the intoxication effect is still active, moves your Zoi further through the night:

  1. Feelin' Good · 1 drink. The first buzz. Your Zoi is settling into the evening, and everything still seems like a perfectly reasonable idea.
  2. Tipsy · 2 drinks. Apparently, one wasn't the plan. The effects build, and your Zoi moves into the next emotional stage.
  3. Drunk · 3 drinks. The night has officially picked up a plot. Intoxication becomes more pronounced, with its own emotional effects and social possibilities.
  4. Wasted · 4 drinks. That escalated quickly. Your Zoi is deep into heavy intoxication, and the aftermath starts to matter.
  5. Blackout · 5 drinks. The final, most severe stage brings blackout behavior and serious risks. Continuing to drink here can have consequences far beyond a rough morning.

Once the intoxication progression ends, a later drinking session starts again at Feelin' Good.

🎭 You can see it on their face

Every stage has its own custom emotional effects and portrait expressions. As the drinks add up, you can follow the progression through your Zoi's changing mood and portrait. The evening has a face of its own.

💬 A little less filtered

Check the social menus after a drink. TipsyZois adds custom drunk interactions, and your Zoi's intoxication stage helps determine which ones you can use.

A quiet drink with a friend, a crowded party, a night out that was supposed to end hours ago: those are all very different scenes to put your Zoi in. See where the interactions take your story.

🤕 Morning has entered the chat

Heavy drinking can leave your Zoi with a hangover after the intoxication wears off, complete with its own emotional effects. Last night's decisions can follow them into the next part of their day.

Very inconvenient for a Zoi who had every intention of being productive.

🚗 The drive home matters

Driving while impaired carries a risk of vehicle crashes. Deciding to drive can change how your Zoi's night ends, with consequences that reach beyond the party.

☠️ When the night goes too far

Extreme drinking can cause alcohol poisoning, which can be fatal. Reaching the most severe stages and continuing to drink puts your Zoi at risk of an outcome a good night's sleep won't fix.

TipsyZois makes room for darker stories alongside the messy nights out. How far you take your Zoi matters.

🎮 Give your save something to talk about

Throw a party for the friend group. Follow the character whose nights always seem to run long. Play the aftermath of an evening that got away from everyone. The mod's custom traits offer another starting point for the characters you create.

Start with an eligible Zoi and one of the game's alcoholic mixed drinks, then watch their emotions and explore the social menus as the stages change. The game's existing age restrictions still apply.

Set the scene, see how far the evening goes, and decide what your Zoi does with the morning after. ♡
