Meet TipsyZois.

An inZOI mod that gives alcoholic mixed drinks a gameplay progression, from an initial buzz to heavier intoxication and its aftermath.

RELEASED · inZOI
✧ tipsyzois.features GAMEPLAY OVERVIEW

Inside TipsyZois ♡

Five stages of intoxication

  1. Feelin' Good
  2. Tipsy
  3. Drunk
  4. Wasted
  5. Blackout

Further drinks advance the stages while intoxication is active.

Emotions & interactions

The stages bring custom emotional effects and portrait expressions. Drinking-related social interactions vary with intoxication.

The aftermath

Heavy drinking can leave a hangover. Impaired driving can cause a crash, and extreme consumption can lead to alcohol poisoning or death.
