Elsewhere Messenger

I missed buddy lists and chat windows with a little character. So I made Elsewhere Messenger, a Windows chat app with glass skins you can design yourself, Creative Studio, Pixel Doll Studio, and a place for your friends to find you.

Before you open a chat

  1. Open the Windows beta build if you already have it installed. For source and project updates, see the Elsewhere Messenger repository folder.
  2. To use online Elsewhere messaging, create an Elsewhere account. Complete the age check, accept the Terms, and request a code.
  3. Open Messenger and choose I have a code. Enter the same email address and the code, then set your handle and display name.
  4. Use New DM or Add contact to find someone by exact handle. Once they accept, you can talk.

What connects where

  • Elsewhere handles your account, friend requests, and online DMs.
  • Instagram shows conversations available through your connection. Use Instagram itself for a new conversation if it is not already in Messenger.
  • Discord supports bot-accessible server channels inside Messenger. Personal DMs open in Discord.
  • Spotify can show now playing when playback access is available.

When something doesn’t connect

  • If Accounts says it is not configured, check that you are using a build connected to the Elsewhere beta.
  • If a code does not arrive, start with the email checklist.
  • If an integration fails, note the name of the integration and its exact error. Email elsewhere.os.info@gmail.com with the app version and what you tried. Leave out tokens, codes, and private chats.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need Elsewhere OS installed?

No. Elsewhere Messenger is a separate desktop app for Windows.

Where do I get an installer?

Download the newest Windows installer (v0.2.49) from the official Elsewhere releases. After that, updates arrive automatically, or use Help, then Check for Updates.

Is this a finished public release?

No. Elsewhere Messenger is in beta (v0.2.49), so features may change and occasional issues are possible. The sign-up website is live.

Do I need an account for everything?

You can use the local Messenger experience without an Elsewhere account. The account is for online Elsewhere contacts, friend requests, and direct messages.

What if my verification email never arrives?

Check the address and your spam folder, then request a fresh code after the app allows you to retry. Use the latest code. If it still fails, contact elsewhere.os.info@gmail.com with the on-screen error. Keep codes and passwords private.

How do I add someone?

Sign in, choose Add contact or New DM under Elsewhere, and enter their exact handle. They need to accept your friend request before you can start an online DM.

Can I switch between accounts?

The Windows beta can remember multiple sign-ins. Use the account switcher to change accounts, or Forget to remove a remembered sign-in from the device.

Are online messages end-to-end encrypted?

Online Elsewhere messages are stored by the service and are not end-to-end encrypted. Conversations are limited to participants through the app. Read the Privacy Policy for the rest.

Can I read personal Discord DMs here?

Personal DMs open in Discord. Messenger can show and send to server channels available to the bot you connected. Both the bot and the channel permissions matter.

Why did Discord return Missing Permissions?

Check whether the bot is in that server and has access to view and send messages in that specific channel. Channel overrides can change what the bot can do.

Why are some Instagram contacts missing?

Messenger can show the conversations available through the connected Instagram API. In the Instagram connection panel, load more if another page is available. A conversation with unknown follow status can appear under Instagram Chats.

Can I start a new Instagram chat inside Messenger?

New DM can open a conversation already available to Messenger. For a new Instagram conversation, Messenger hands off to Instagram.

Does Spotify play music inside Messenger?

The connection shows playback status when Spotify grants access. If it says playback unavailable, check the account and the access attached to your Spotify app.

How do I hide notification previews?

Open Tools, then Desktop Notifications, and turn off message previews. Notification visibility also depends on your Windows settings.

Can I change the Messenger look?

Open Tools, then Skins. Classic is the blue-glass look and the default. The official collections (Neapolitan Dream, Mint Choco Diary, and Frutiger Evil, which includes Dead Signal) each open as a folder. Skins you design in SkinMapper appear under My Skins.

Do I need a webcam for Creative Studio?

No. Open Activities, then Creative Studio. Start from a blank canvas or import a picture, then add stickers, frames, text and filters. Use the Camera Booth to take a new photo or a photo strip if your camera is available and allowed.

What is Pixel Doll Studio?

A layered dress-up studio for pixel dolls, in beta. Open Activities, then Pixel Doll Studio. Pick a body, skin tone and pose, add hair, makeup and outfits, then save your doll or export a PNG. The doll art is still being drawn, so the beta uses simple placeholder shapes for now.

Can I nudge an Instagram or Discord contact?

Nudges are an Elsewhere messaging feature. External integrations keep their own messaging controls.

Where do Call and Mail go?

Call opens the service you choose, such as Discord, Instagram, or the Windows phone handler. Mail opens your default email compose handler.

How do I delete my account or report a problem?

Open Tools, then Elsewhere Accounts & Messages, to delete your account in the app, or contact elsewhere.os.info@gmail.com. For a problem with a person in Messenger, use its report or block controls where available. Signing out only ends your session.
