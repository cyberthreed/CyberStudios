✧ A COZY DESKTOP COMPANION · PREVIEW ✧

Your cozy corner, on your desktop.

bowl.rip is a Windows desktop companion about slowing down. Pack, light, bubble and exhale with your favorite illustrated glass, then decorate a lounge of your own and build a collection over time.

Everything stays on your device: no accounts, no analytics, no cloud sync. This page is a static preview with concept art, since the app isn't released yet. ♡

♡ what_it_does.txt PREVIEW FEATURES

What it does

A summary of the current 0.3.0 preview. Features may change before a public release.

Three companion looks

Glass only, Perch or Full widget. Drag it anywhere on your desktop, scale the glass from 75 to 200%, and give each glass its own remembered spot.

A lounge to decorate

Nine backgrounds and 48 decorations. Move, resize, rotate, layer and snap furniture, then save up to five room presets.

A collection to grow

24 glasses, 24 cosmetic strains and 14 lighters, with a Glass Workshop for mixing compatible parts.

Creative extras

Smoke Painter, room Polaroids, stargazing, per-glass mastery and visits from Byte, who is not always helpful.

Gentle comforts

Named reminders with quiet hours, synthesized ambient sounds, your own MP3 or WAV tracks, a sleep timer, and private notes and doodles.

Private by design

Saves live on your computer. No accounts, analytics, cloud sync or automatic startup, and sound starts off.
