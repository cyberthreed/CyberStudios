? help_and_questions.txt FAQ

A few little questions

Is CyberStudios the same thing as Byte.exe?

No. CyberStudios is the parent studio. Byte.exe is one of its creations and the first desktop companion in the Bytes product line.

Is Cybercore the same as CyberCore Gameplay?

No. Cybercore refers to a dreamy digital aesthetic used in projects such as the Cotton Candy VS Code themes. CyberCore Gameplay is a separate inZOI gameplay framework in development.

Does Byte.exe need an account?

No. Byte.exe is a local-first desktop companion. Its care state is stored on your computer.

Is Byte.exe an AI chatbot?

No. Byte uses locally defined interactions and responses. His Talk feature does not require an online AI provider.

Where can I download Byte.exe?

Visit the official Byte.exe page for the Windows download and instructions.
