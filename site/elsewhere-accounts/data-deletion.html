Delete your Elsewhere data

Effective date: November 21, 2026

Delete your Elsewhere account

  1. Open Elsewhere Messenger and sign in.
  2. Go to Tools → Elsewhere Accounts & Messages.
  3. Choose Delete account…, enter your password and confirm.

This permanently deletes your Elsewhere account and everything stored for it on our servers: your email sign-in, handle, display name, contacts, blocks, reports you filed, feedback you sent, photos you sent and every Elsewhere message you sent or received. It also removes the Instagram and Discord sign-ins saved on that PC. Deletion happens right away and can't be undone.

Forgot your password? Use Forgot password on the sign-in screen to set a new one, then follow the steps above.

Instagram data

Elsewhere doesn't store your Instagram messages on its servers. They're loaded from Instagram straight into the app on your PC. For instant updates, the server keeps which Instagram account is connected to your Elsewhere account, plus short "new message arrived" notices with no message content, which are deleted within a day.

  • To stop using Instagram in Elsewhere, go to Tools → Social Accounts & Settings → Instagram Connection and choose Disconnect.
  • To remove Elsewhere's access on Meta's side, open Facebook Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations and remove Elsewhere Messenger.
  • Deleting your Elsewhere account removes the connection record too.

Discord data

Your Discord sign-in is saved only on your PC, in Windows Credential Manager. Disconnect it in Tools → Social Accounts & Settings → Manage Discord, or remove Elsewhere under Discord's User Settings → Authorized Apps.
