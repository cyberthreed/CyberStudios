This policy covers Elsewhere Accounts and the connected features that use them. Local Messenger conversations remain on your device unless you choose a feature that transmits data, such as Elsewhere Accounts or a third-party integration.

What we collect

  • Signup and account: Email, verified email status, account ID, handle, display name, Terms version and time accepted by the verified account, an initial signup acknowledgement, and an eligibility flag. An eligible registrant’s date of birth is processed transiently for the age check and is not saved in the account database. An ineligible visitor is not asked for an email.
  • Bot and security checks: The bot-check provider processes technical signals needed to check the request. Elsewhere uses service and security records to prevent spam and protect accounts. These may include timestamps, IP address, security events, and limited usage records held by our service providers.
  • Messaging and safety: Friend requests, friendship and block status, message content, message participants, timestamps, and reports submitted to support. Only participants can access their conversations through the normal app. Online messages are not end-to-end encrypted. Authorized staff or service providers may access the minimum information necessary for support, investigation, security, or legal obligations.
  • On this device: Windows Credential Manager can retain a refresh credential if you choose to remember your account. A local index stores remembered email labels and active-account preference. Optional desktop notifications can show sender names and message previews on the screen or in Windows Notification Center. You can disable previews in Messenger.

How we use and share information

We process information to create and verify accounts, deliver messages, show friend relationships, maintain security, handle reports, enforce Terms, and respond to requests and legal obligations. Your email is used for account access and service communication. Other users can see your handle and display name through permitted account lookup and messaging. Your email is not shown in the contact directory.

When the planned providers are enabled, Supabase hosts account, authentication and message data; Cloudflare Turnstile checks automated signup; and [EMAIL PROVIDER] delivers email verification codes. Provider configuration, processing locations, and international safeguards: [CONFIRMED PROVIDERS, LOCATIONS AND SAFEGUARDS]. When you choose an integration like Instagram or Discord, the provider handles requests under its own policy. We may disclose information when legally required or necessary to investigate serious abuse or protect someone.

We do not plan to sell account data or use private messages for targeted advertising. Confirm the final production services and verify these commitments before publishing this policy.

Retention and deletion

We keep account and messaging information for the time necessary to provide the account or as otherwise required for safety, legal obligations, or resolving disputes. Before launch, publish the actual retention windows for unverified accounts, active messages and closed accounts, security and rate-limit logs, reports, and expiring backups: [RETENTION SCHEDULE].

You can request access, correction, export, or deletion at the privacy contact above. We may verify that the request comes from the account owner. Deleting an account affects copies in the service according to the published schedule. It cannot retract screenshots, exports, or other copies people have already made. Required or limited data may be retained for legal, safety, or backup purposes as explained in the final schedule.

Children, your rights, and contact

Elsewhere Accounts are for people 13 and older, subject to applicable local restrictions. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact elsewhere.os.info@gmail.com so we can investigate and remove information as appropriate.

You may have additional rights under applicable law, including a right to complain to a privacy regulator. Where legally required, the final policy will name the responsible organization, applicable legal bases, required regional disclosures, and contact details. We will post policy changes and provide additional notice when required.
