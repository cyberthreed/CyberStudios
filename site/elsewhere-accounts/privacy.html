Elsewhere Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026
Who operates Elsewhere: Elsewhere OS / CyberStudios
Privacy requests: elsewhere.os.info@gmail.com

This policy covers Elsewhere Accounts and the connected features that use them. Local Messenger conversations remain on your device unless you choose a feature that transmits data, such as Elsewhere Accounts or a third-party integration.

What we collect

  • Signup and account: Email, verified email status, account ID, handle, display name, Terms version and time accepted by the verified account, an initial signup acknowledgement, and an eligibility flag. The signup form checks your date of birth in your browser before asking for an email. If you continue and submit the form, the signup function checks the date again and saves an eligibility flag in the account record. The signup function does not save the date of birth in the account database.
  • Bot and security checks: The bot-check provider processes technical signals needed to check the request. Elsewhere uses service and security records to prevent spam and protect accounts. These may include timestamps, IP address, security events, and limited usage records held by our service providers.
  • Messaging and safety: Friend requests, friendship and block status, message content (including photos you send, which are stored privately and can only be opened by you and the person you sent them to, and links to GIFs you send), message participants, timestamps, and reports submitted to support. Only participants can access their conversations through the normal app. Online messages are not end-to-end encrypted. Authorized staff or service providers may access the minimum information necessary for support, investigation, security, or legal obligations.
  • Online status and typing: While Elsewhere Messenger is open, your accepted friends can see whether you are online, away or busy (unless you choose Appear Offline) and when you are typing to them. This passes through our realtime service and is not stored.
  • Feedback: If you use Help → Send Feedback, we store what you write, the app version and skin, linked to your account, so we can fix problems and follow up.
  • On this device: Windows Credential Manager can retain a refresh credential if you choose to remember your account. A local index stores remembered email labels and active-account preference. Optional desktop notifications can show sender names and message previews on the screen or in Windows Notification Center. You can disable previews in Messenger.

Instagram, Discord and other connections in Elsewhere Messenger

  • Instagram: If you connect an Instagram professional (Business or Creator) account, you sign in through Meta. Your Instagram sign-in is saved only on your PC, in Windows Credential Manager, under your Elsewhere account. The app loads your Instagram direct messages, the people you message with and their profile details (such as name, username and profile picture) straight from Meta so you can read and reply, and sends a message only when you choose to send it. Elsewhere does not keep copies of your Instagram messages on its servers. To finish sign-in, an Elsewhere server function briefly handles the one-time code from Meta and exchanges it for your sign-in without saving it. For instant message updates, Elsewhere keeps which Instagram account is connected to your Elsewhere account, plus short activity notices from Meta that contain no message content and are deleted within a day.
  • Discord and other optional services: Their sign-ins are saved only on your PC, in Windows Credential Manager. The app contacts the service directly to show your conversations and send messages you write. GIF searches go to GIPHY.
  • Updates: Elsewhere Messenger checks GitHub for new versions, so GitHub receives that request, including your IP address.
  • Disconnecting: You can disconnect Instagram or Discord at any time in Elsewhere Messenger under Tools → Social Accounts & Settings. You can also remove Elsewhere Messenger from your Meta or Discord account settings.

How we use and share information

We process information to create and verify accounts, deliver messages, show friend relationships, maintain security, handle reports, enforce Terms, and respond to requests and legal obligations. Your email is used for account access and service communication. Other users can see your handle and display name through permitted account lookup and messaging. Your email is not shown in the contact directory.

GitHub Pages hosts the signup website and records visitor IP addresses for security. Supabase hosts Elsewhere’s account, authentication, and messaging data. Cloudflare Turnstile checks for automated signups using signals from your browser. The signup function sends Cloudflare the challenge token for verification. The email address and date of birth are handled by the signup function and are not included in that verification request. Supabase Auth uses the configured mail provider to send one-time verification codes. Supabase hosts the primary database in Oregon, United States (AWS us-west-2). Providers may process information outside your location to deliver, secure, and support the service. See Supabase’s data residency and transfers information and Cloudflare’s Turnstile privacy notice. When you choose an integration like Instagram or Discord, the provider handles requests under its own policy. We may disclose information when legally required or necessary to investigate serious abuse or protect someone.

We do not plan to sell account data or use private messages for targeted advertising.

Retention and deletion

We keep account and messaging information for the time necessary to provide the account or as otherwise required for safety, legal obligations, or resolving disputes. Elsewhere does not currently have a fixed retention schedule. Unverified registrations may remain until removed. Active account and message data may remain while the account is open. Security records, reports, and backup copies may have separate retention periods based on their purpose and provider settings.

You can delete your Elsewhere account yourself in Elsewhere Messenger under Tools → Elsewhere Accounts & Messages → Delete account. See how to delete your data.

You can request access, correction, export, or deletion at the privacy contact above. We may verify that the request comes from the account owner. Account deletion applies to records in the live service as described above. It cannot retract screenshots, exports, or other copies people have already made. Limited copies may remain for legal, safety, or backup purposes.

Children, your rights, and contact

Elsewhere Accounts are for people 13 and older, subject to applicable local restrictions. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information, contact elsewhere.os.info@gmail.com so we can investigate and remove information as appropriate.

You may have additional rights under applicable law, including a right to complain to a privacy regulator. We will post policy changes and provide additional notice when required.
