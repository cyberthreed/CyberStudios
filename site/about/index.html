♡ about the studio

CyberStudios is my personal creative technology studio, a home for all the things I love making and all the ideas that refuse to stay inside my head.

I work across game development, AI systems, 3D art, digital design, and creative coding. Sometimes that means building something ambitious like CyberCore. Other times, it means spending hours making a tiny digital cat feel at home on someone's desktop.

I love technology that has personality. Interfaces that feel nostalgic. Software that invites you to explore. Digital worlds that feel like they belong to someone.

CyberStudios is my way of bringing those things together, one experiment at a time. ♡

✧ creative_direction.txt STUDIO NOTES

Software with a personality of its own.

I believe software can be colorful. Strange. Nostalgic. Unexpected. A little surreal. It can make you curious, keep you company, or turn the place where you work into somewhere you enjoy being.

CyberStudios is where I explore those possibilities through games, creative AI, digital art, customization, and unusual little applications. Some projects ask how a virtual world could feel more connected. Others simply give a small pixel cat somewhere to nap.

Imagination, individuality, and experimentation run through all of it. The tools and projects can have different purposes while sharing the same instinct: make technology feel more expressive, personal, and playful.

♡ studio_residents.txtCY & BYTE

Where Cy creates, Byte follows. ♡

✦

Cy · resident digital dreamer

Cy is the face and creative mascot of CyberStudios. She represents imagination, individuality, experimentation, and the belief that technology can be expressive. The artist at the window, with another idea waiting to become something.

Byte · professional screen dweller

Our official black-and-purple pixel cat, with glowing cyan eyes and a star on his forehead. His favorite activities include sleeping, collecting pixels, and keeping Cy company. Byte.exe is his own cozy desktop home, and the first release in the Bytes family.

✦ studio_disciplines.dat WHAT WE CREATE

The creative directory

CyberStudios explores several connected disciplines. Each has room to develop its own personality.

🎮

Games & gameplay systems

Experimental mechanics, immersive storytelling, and ideas for more adaptive experiences. This is where the studio explores how virtual worlds can carry more context and personality.

🧠

Creative AI

Exploring artificial intelligence as part of interactive worlds and software. These experiments sit alongside the studio’s other projects; Byte’s own conversations use local, predefined phrases.

🎨

Digital art & design

3D art, original characters, interface experiments, and visual storytelling. Cy and Byte belong to that same interest in making a digital identity feel like someone lives there.

💻

Creative coding

Widgets, web experiments, desktop tools, and unusual little applications. Ideas get room to become useful, playful, or pleasantly hard to categorize.

🌸

Customization

Themes, icons, wallpapers, sprites, and palettes that make technology feel personal. Cybercore Cotton Candy brings some of those colors into the place where you write code.

💿

Internet nostalgia

Personal websites, virtual pets, playful digital spaces, and the feeling of finding a forgotten page at 2 AM. A little of that old-web curiosity, carried into new things.

⌘ identity_reference.txt DESIGN LANGUAGE

One universe. Different identities.

The studio’s Cybercore aesthetic draws from pastel vaporwave, dreamy Y2K technology, liminal digital spaces, nostalgic interfaces, and Frutiger Aero. Baby pink, lavender, powder blue, and pearly white. Floating windows, dreamy skies, old computers, and virtual worlds.

VISUAL AESTHETIC

Cybercore

The studio’s visual language: pastel vaporwave, nostalgic computing, soft digital environments, and a fondness for interfaces that feel like places. It appears in the Wiki, artwork, themes, and other creative projects.

GAMEPLAY TECHNOLOGY

CyberCore Gameplay

A separate, developing framework for inZOI gameplay modules, shared context, memory, and compatible integrations.

These names have different meanings. The studio's visual aesthetic is not the same product as the CyberCore Gameplay framework.
? studio_faq.txt QUESTIONS

A few little questions.

Is CyberStudios just a desktop pet project?

No. Byte.exe is one CyberStudios creation. The studio also includes creative tools, gameplay systems, and experimental software projects.

Are all CyberStudios projects connected?

Not necessarily. Projects share a studio identity but can remain independent applications or systems. Technical integrations depend on each project's design.

What is the relationship between Byte and the Bytes?

Bytes is the digital companion product line. Byte.exe features the first Byte, a pixel cat named Byte.

Where can I explore the source code?

Visit the CyberStudios GitHub repository to browse available project source code and documentation.
