# ♡ a little kindness in cyberspace ✧

**`dream in pixels. create without limits.`**

☁️　♡　🫧　✧

CyberStudios welcomes curious people, creative experiments, questions, and constructive disagreement. Help make the official project spaces somewhere people can participate with dignity.

---

### 🌸 how we show up

- Be respectful across backgrounds, identities, abilities, and levels of experience.
- Give useful feedback about code, design, behavior, or ideas. Explain the concern and leave room for a response.
- Assume a question can be asked in good faith. Offer clear guidance when someone is learning.
- Respect boundaries, privacy, attribution, and other people's time.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and make a reasonable effort to repair the impact.

Disagreement is allowed. Personal attacks are not.

### ⛔ what doesn't belong here

Harassment, discriminatory abuse, threats, sexual harassment, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, impersonation, malicious contributions, and deliberate disruption are not welcome.

Do not publish someone else's private information, credentials, or private conversations without permission. Do not use project spaces to intimidate people or target them elsewhere.

### 🫧 where this applies

This policy applies to official CyberStudios repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces, and to people representing the project in an official capacity. Independent forks manage their own communities.

### ✉️ raising a concern

For a concern that can be described safely in public, you can flag it to **@cyberthreed** in the relevant project thread or issue.

For a sensitive conduct concern, use a private contact route the maintainer has already shared with you. If you don't have one, open a brief [issue](https://github.com/cyberthreed/CyberStudios/issues) requesting a private way to contact the maintainer. Keep names, evidence, and incident details out of that public request. Reports about GitHub platform abuse can also be made through GitHub's reporting controls.

The private vulnerability form is for **security vulnerabilities**; see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for that process.

### ⌘ how maintainers respond

Maintainers consider the context, severity, and pattern of behavior. They may ask for a change, give a warning, remove disruptive content, limit participation, or remove access to official project spaces using the platform's available controls.

People involved should have a reasonable opportunity to explain their perspective when doing so is safe and practical. Report details will be handled with care and shared only as needed to assess or address the concern; absolute confidentiality cannot be promised.

If you believe a moderation decision missed important context, ask the maintainer for a review through an available private contact route. Retaliation against someone for raising a concern is not acceptable.

---

**Be curious about the work. Be considerate of the people making it.** ♡
