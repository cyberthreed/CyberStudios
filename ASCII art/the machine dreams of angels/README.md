## 𓆩♡𓆪 about

**the machine dreams of angels** is an interactive, animated ASCII art experience built with Python, JavaScript, HTML, and CSS.

The project translates gothic sigilism illustration into thousands of individually rendered characters, reconstructing  wings, long hair, AN ornate sword, thorn covered structures, and intricate linework through code.

What began as a challenge to recreate an existing piece of artwork using ASCII evolved into an experimental digital exhibition exploring the relationship between **traditional art, creative coding, and computational interpretation.**

The objective was not to generate a new angel.

It was to preserve the identity of my original illustration while giving it an entirely different medium of expression.

> *even sleeping machines remember the sky.*

---

## ☾ the exhibition

A gothic apparition emerges from darkness, its form reconstructed through shifting typographic marks and muted light.

**Features**

- ♡ **Animated ASCII rendering:** Individual glyphs form the evolving image.
- ◇ **Atmospheric colors:** Lavender, dusty pink, icy blue, and near-black.
- ⌘ **Python rendering backend:** Supplies character and color information to the browser.
- ✦ **Interactive visualization:** Adjustable motion, glyph density, and glow.
- ☾ **Custom exhibition UI:** A minimal, responsive interface designed around the artwork.
- ♡ **Reduced-motion support:** Respects the viewer's motion preferences.

The exhibition uses actual typographic glyphs rendered through the Canvas API rather than placing a conventional image behind a text overlay.

## ⚙ behind the machine

**Technologies**

`Python` · `JavaScript` · `HTML5` · `CSS3` · `Canvas API`

Each displayed character contributes to the image's structure, shading, and movement.

The frontend renders glyphs using a character atlas and applies optional glow to create the artwork's luminous appearance.

## 𓆩✧𓆪 artistic direction

The original illustration combines monochromatic gothic fantasy, ornamental linework, and elaborate engraved textures.

Its visual identity is defined by the contrast between delicate details and dramatic silhouettes.

The recreation explores how these elements translate into a character-based system.

**Can individual symbols preserve the movement of hair?**

**Can typography recreate a feather's texture?**

**Can something made of data still carry the emotional atmosphere of the original artwork?**

These are the questions behind the project.

It treats ASCII as an artistic material rather than merely a stylistic filter.

---

## ♡ creative philosophy

My work often exists somewhere between software development and digital art.

I'm interested in creating things that don't fit neatly into one category: programs that behave like artworks, interfaces that become fictional spaces, and digital experiences that invite exploration.

**the machine dreams of angels** is an experiment in translating artistic intent through computational systems.

The original illustration becomes data.

The data becomes typography.

The typography becomes an animated image.

And throughout the transformation, the original composition remains at its center.

> *The computer does not need to understand the angel to reproduce its form.*
