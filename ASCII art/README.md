# ✧ ascii apparition exhibition ♡

*somewhere between a forgotten computer and a daydream.*

Welcome to my corner of ASCII art! ♡

This is where I experiment with turning images, characters, and strange ideas into artwork made entirely from text.

Some pieces move. Some glow. Some feel like something you weren't supposed to find running on an old computer at 2 AM.

**Made of characters, code, and a little digital haunting.**

### ☾ the exhibition

**#00 · THE MACHINE DREAMS OF ANGELS**

*something sacred inside obsolete hardware.*

An animated ASCII apparition exploring light, movement, and the strange beauty of something almost divine taking shape inside a machine.

♡ [View the interactive exhibition](https://cyberthreed.github.io/CyberStudios/ascii-art/exhibition/00-the-machine-dreams-of-angels/)
