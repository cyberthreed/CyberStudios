### 🐈‍⬛ meet byte!

```text
  /\_/\
 ( o.o )  ♡
  > ^ <
```

**Name:** Byte

**Occupation:** Professional screen dweller

**Favorite activities:** Sleeping, collecting pixels, and keeping Cy company.

**Special ability:** Making absolutely everything cuter.

Byte is CyberStudios' official mascot, a tiny black-and-purple pixel cat with glowing cyan eyes, a star on his forehead, and a personality all his own.

He's the star of **Byte.exe**, CyberStudios' first released desktop companion! ♡

Give him a home on your Windows desktop, play with him, keep him company, or let him wander around while you're coding, creating, and collecting far too many open tabs.

**Byte.exe v1.0.0 is available now.** Your newest screen dweller is waiting to meet you. 🐾

*Please do not feed the computer after midnight. Byte has questions.*

### ✧ brand aesthetic: cybercore

**Cybercore** is our original creative aesthetic inspired by pastel vaporwave, dreamy Y2K technology, liminal digital spaces, nostalgic interfaces, and Frutiger Aero influences.

Think:

`baby pink` ♡ `lavender` ♡ `powder blue` ♡ `pearly white`

Floating windows. Pixel companions. Dreamy skies. Old computers. Virtual worlds. The feeling of discovering a forgotten website at 2 AM.

*An internet that feels alive again.*

**Note:** Our *Cybercore aesthetic* and *CyberCore gameplay framework* are two distinct parts of the CyberStudios universe.
